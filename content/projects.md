## RAMSES Farinella

<!--
In the RAMSES Farinella mission row, below the project-phase label. Blank lines create
separate paragraphs.
-->

Designed and validated the navigation filter for the RAMSES RCS-1 (Farinella) CubeSat,
combining centroiding and LiDAR measurements.

## FUTURE

<!--
In the FUTURE mission row, below the project-phase label. Blank lines create separate
paragraphs.
-->

Designed and integrated the navigation filter and image-processing interfaces for
autonomous visual navigation on the ASI FUTURE payload. Planned, ran, and analysed
open-loop camera and Jetson Orin NX hardware-in-the-loop tests in the RETINA and
TinyV3RSE optical facilities.
