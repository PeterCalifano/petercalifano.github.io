01 / RESEARCH

Research interests

Visual navigation, mapping, and multi-sensor state estimation for spacecraft and robots operating with limited prior knowledge of their surroundings.

Navigation & mapping

PRIMARY RESEARCH

SLAM for small-body exploration

  • Joint estimation of spacecraft motion and environmental geometry from visual observations.
  • Integration of factor-graph smoothing, recursive filtering, and orbital dynamics for proximity navigation.
  • Metric-scale observability and uncertainty under target rotation, variable illumination, and uncertain dynamics.
  • Computational efficiency and estimation consistency within onboard processing constraints.
  • Visual SLAM
  • Factor graphs
  • Recursive filtering
  • Orbital dynamics

Multi-sensor estimation

ROBOTICS

State estimation for robotic platforms

  • Tightly coupled fusion of visual, inertial, joint-state, and contact measurements.
  • Motion estimation for humanoids and rovers with free-floating or underactuated bases.
  • Complementary geometric, kinematic, and contact constraints for localization.
  • Assessment of sensor contributions, estimation accuracy, and uncertainty.
  • Sensor fusion
  • Inertial sensing
  • Contact measurements
  • Visual navigation

Perception

VISION & LEARNING

Visual measurements for navigation

  • Feature tracking and multi-view geometry for motion estimation and scene reconstruction.
  • Photometric modelling of visual measurements under variable illumination and viewing geometry.
  • Learning-based correction of image-derived navigation measurements, including target centroid estimates.
  • Event-based vision for odometry and localization.
  • Computer vision
  • Photometric modelling
  • Machine learning
  • Event-based vision

Simulation & validation

SIMULATION & VALIDATION

Modelling and evaluation of navigation systems

  • Spacecraft dynamics models with gravity, solar-radiation pressure, and manoeuvres.
  • Synthetic sensor measurements for navigation-system development and validation.
  • Spectral and radiometric rendering under variable illumination, surface properties, and viewing geometry.
  • Monte Carlo assessment of estimation errors, uncertainty, and computational cost.
  • Dynamical modelling
  • Sensor simulation
  • Physically based rendering
  • Monte Carlo analysis
